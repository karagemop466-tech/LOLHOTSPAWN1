  • Why we don't fall back to Hotspawn. The Hotspawn lobby shows a crowd pick percentage. That is a popularity poll, not a price, and the brief excludes it. Publishing it as a probability would be misleading.
  • What would resolve them. A verified bookmaker line (any regulated sportsbook or esports book), a liquid prediction market, or an official league results feed strong enough to originate a line. When one of these appears, the match moves to Picks automatically on the next refresh.
