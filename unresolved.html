Documented limitations across the list

These are the limits of generating a pick for every match. Nothing here is silently smoothed over: the affected rows are marked in the list and counted above.

LimitationScopeImpact & handling
Mid-split league removal (dqteams)4 matchesLeaguepedia's official Data: pages record that Solid Gaming was removed in Round 10 of Komplettligaen 2026 Autumn and remaining matches were forfeited. Hotspawn may void the card or grade the walkover, so a competitive win probability would be fabricated. If Hotspawn settles walkovers, the active opponent named on each row receives the default win.
No reachable sportsbook market232 matches31 bookmaker, esportsbook and aggregator endpoints were probed from the build runner. 13 answered, but only 1 served parsed priced LoL markets, and those cover the top tier only. National/academy divisions are not offered by Pinnacle, Bovada, Kalshi or Polymarket, and the specialist books that might price them are geo-fenced or bot-protected from the runner. The full evidence table is below.
Thin match history79 provisionalAt least one side has fewer than five rated series before kick-off. The line is still published, but labelled provisional and included in the calibration audit.
No history at all before kick-off2 matchesA newly formed or newly promoted team has no rated series before kick-off; the 1500 prior is used and the row is flagged.
Name disambiguation14 matchesHomonymous regional teams (e.g. 'Fenrir (Icelandic Team)' vs Filipino/Swedish Fenrir; Hotspawn's 'Phong Vu Buffalo' label on a Swiss PvB slot) are joined only with competition-scoped evidence, logged on Verification and Flagged.
No common opponent before kick-off21 matchesThe pair is linked only through different branches of the opponent graph, so the two ratings are less directly comparable. Out-of-sample these matches are weaker (61.0% accuracy, 0.228 Brier versus 66.6% / 0.211 when a common opponent exists); they are flagged and given low confidence rather than being silently treated as solid.
Stale Hotspawn cards65 matchesHotspawn keeps returning status='scheduled' for fixtures whose start time has passed. They are kept so coverage matches the site exactly, marked 'started', and priced from the pre-kick-off rating snapshot.
Community wiki sourceall model rowsLeaguepedia is a volunteer-maintained wiki, not a Riot Games property. Riot's official LoL Esports API is used only to cross-check the World Championship schedule because it does not publish these divisions.
