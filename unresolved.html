Documented limitations across the list

LimitationScopeImpact & handling
Mid-split league removals (dqteams)4 matchesLeaguepedia's Data: page for the competition records the removal (for Komplettligaen, Round 10 carries the footnote '[[Solid Gaming]] is removed from the league.' and every remaining series is scored 0-2 with an ff flag). Because Hotspawn may either void the card or grade a walkover for the remaining active opponent, an Elo probability built from pre-removal results is invalid, so the row is quarantined and only the official settlement winner is shown.
No sportsbook market on national divisions232 matchesOnly the competitions listed on the Market page have a reachable price in this snapshot. The remaining national, academy and third-division leagues are not offered by any book the build runner can reach, so their lines are originated from recorded series history rather than borrowed from a placeholder.
Thin match history77 provisionalAt least one team has fewer than five rated series in the 25-month Leaguepedia window, so the Elo estimate carries wider variance and is explicitly flagged provisional.
One-sided history0 matchesOne side is newly promoted or newly formed with zero prior Leaguepedia series and receives the 1500 prior.
Name disambiguation0 matchesHomonymous regional teams (e.g. 'Fenrir (Icelandic Team)' vs Polish 'Fenrir', 'PvB (Swiss Team)' mislabelled by Hotspawn as Vietnamese 'Phong Vũ Buffalo') are joined only via competition-scoped roster evidence.
Disconnected opponent graphs7 matchesCross-pool teams without a shared opponent chain are flagged rather than treated as high-confidence lines.
Geo-fenced / bot-protected bookssee SourcesEvery book probed on this build, its HTTP status and the reason it could not be used are listed on Sources. A book that returns a page but only renders symmetric 1.8/1.8 placeholders (EGamersWorld) is never treated as a price.
