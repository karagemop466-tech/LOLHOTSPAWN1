Limitations of this method

No published price exists for most of these leagues

Sub-tier LoL (national 2nd/3rd divisions, regional championships, academy leagues) is not listed by Polymarket and is only sporadically listed by sportsbook odds-comparison pages. Where no price exists the row falls back to our own Elo line; where not even that exists it is listed as unresolved.

Hotspawn's own pick share is excluded on purpose

The lobby's “X% picked” is a crowd-vote percentage, not a probability, and the brief rules it out as a probability source. It is shown next to our number for comparison only and is never an input.

Roster, patch and veto information is not published for these tiers

Our own line is a form/strength model. It cannot account for roster swaps, stand-ins or patch swings in amateur leagues, which is why low-sample rows are labelled “very low” confidence rather than being presented as a priced line.

Data is collected and rebuilt by GitHub Actions every 6 hours (.github/workflows/refresh.yml) — no manual step. Probabilities come from external betting lines or our own model, never from Hotspawn's pick share.
