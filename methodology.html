The 4-tier probability architecture

  1. Inventory. Every League of Legends fixture is pulled from Hotspawn's public fixtures API (play.hotspawn.com/lol-pickems).
  2. Tier 1 — Multi-venue real-money consensus. Live odds and contract books from Pinnacle (0.36), Polymarket full-series CLOB (0.24), Kalshi CFTC (0.18), Bovada (0.12) and Unibet/Kambi (0.10) are de-vigged proportionally and precision-weighted. Only series moneylines are accepted: Pinnacle's parallel "(Kills)" derivative is filtered out in collect.py and again in model.py, and archived third-party quotes that cannot be re-verified on the build machine are retired rather than reused.
  3. Tier 2 — Originated regional Elo lines. All recorded Leaguepedia series since MatchSchedule.DateTime_UTC >= '2024-09-01 00:00:00' are processed strictly chronologically with division priors (1700 major / 1575 challenger-tier-2 / 1500 regional / 1455 open-div-3), series margin-of-victory scaling, inactivity decay and empirical Bayes shrinkage toward each team's tier prior.
  4. Tier 3 — Provisional & Unresolved quarantine. Thin-sample teams (<5 series) are marked provisional; officially removed mid-split teams (dqteams) are quarantined in Unresolved rather than mispriced.
  5. Automated publish gate. Every 6 hours GitHub Actions re-runs the full pipeline and executes the publish gate (Verification) before publishing to GitHub Pages; a failing assertion aborts the commit instead of shipping bad data.

Formulas

Expected series win probability for team A:

EA = 1 / (1 + 10^((RB - RA)/400))

Chronological rating update after each completed series (K = 26.0, margin multiplier 1 + 0.18 · max(0, Δgames − 1)):

R′A = RA + Keff · (SA - EA)

Empirical Bayes shrinkage towards tier prior R0 for sample size n (shrink = 5.0):

Reff = R0 + (R - R0) · n / (n + 5.0)

Inactivity decay begins after 45 days and reaches 1500 at 450 days; originated probabilities are clamped to 0.05–0.95.

Hard methodological guardrails

  • Zero Hotspawn crowd input. Crowd pick shares are never read by the probability engine.
  • Zero placeholder odds. Symmetric 1.80 / 1.80 defaults on odds aggregators are discarded, and any quote that cannot be re-fetched on the build machine is excluded from the probability entirely.
  • Official walkovers are not forecasts. Where a league has ruled a forfeit, the settlement winner is published in a separate field with no probability and is excluded from the backtest and from the market-coverage counts.
  • Explicit quarantine over guesswork. Disqualified teams (dqteams) and unrated cards are documented transparently on Unresolved and Flagged for review.
  • Receipts expire. A bookmaker quote captured from a match page is a line only while it is fresh; archived receipts older than 24 hours are excluded from the consensus, so an affected match falls back to the live venues or to the model rather than to a stale price.
  • Market vs model is disclosed. Every market-priced row also publishes our own Elo probability and flags a gap of 20 percentage points or more, instead of quietly blending the two.
  • Explicit quarantine over guesswork. Disqualified teams (dqteams) get a settlement pick with no probability, and every caveat is documented on Unresolved and Flagged for review.
