Methodology

1. Match universe (Hotspawn)

https://play.hotspawn.com/lol-pickems is an Angular SSR page. Its hydrated state is embedded in <script id="ng-state">; the collector reads the match list from that JSON. We take only the fixtures: id, teams, competition, tier, kickoff, points. Crowd percentages are stored for display and are excluded from every calculation.

2. External prices

Three sources are queried on every refresh:

  • Bovada — public pre-match coupon for League of Legends, Head-to-Head moneyline, full series.
  • Pinnacle — guest API, sport 12 (E Sports) filtered to League of Legends leagues, full-series moneyline.
  • Polymarket — Gamma API, league-of-legends tag, open series markets. The traded price is used directly.

Each two-way price is converted to implied probability (1/decimal) and de-vigged proportionally: p = implied / (implied_a + implied_b). Multiple sources are combined with the median, which is robust to a single stale or mispriced book. The published probability is the median for the favourite; dispersion (max − min across sources) sets confidence: ≤5pp high, ≤12pp medium, otherwise low.

3. Matching

Team names are normalised (case, accents, punctuation, and generic suffixes such as “Esports”, “Gaming”, “Team”). A pair matches when both sides match exactly or one name contains the other (e.g. “LYON” / “Lyon Gaming”). Name variants are flagged on the review page. Kickoff timestamps must agree within 48 hours when both sides have one.

4. When nothing is listed

No price means no probability. The match is published on Unresolved with the reason. We do not fall back to Hotspawn crowd data, and we do not interpolate a number to fill the table.

5. Freshness

A GitHub Actions workflow runs every 4 hours (17 */4 * * * UTC), refetches both the lobby and the markets, rebuilds these pages and commits them. The timestamp in the header is the generation time of the snapshot you are reading. Hotspawn adds matches continuously, so a match that appears after a run will show up on the next one (within 4 hours).

6. Known limits

  • Books publish lines close to kickoff. Early in a day the priced-match count is lower.
  • Polymarket prices are last-trade/mid prices, not bookmaker lines; they are labelled per card.
  • Pinnacle’s guest API is undocumented and may change; the Sources page shows its live status each run.
  • No model-driven probabilities are published. Any number shown is traceable to a linked external price.

7. Data files
