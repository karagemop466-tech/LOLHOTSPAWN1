Method

How every probability on this site is computed, backtested, and audited.

Updated 2026-10-23 15:44 UTC · next automatic refresh ~18:17 UTC · 23 checks run before every publish

The probability architecture

  1. Inventory. Every League of Legends fixture is pulled from Hotspawn's public fixtures API (play.hotspawn.com/lol-pickems) on every build, including national and academy divisions.
  2. Bookmaker / exchange first. Live odds and contract books from Pinnacle, Polymarket full-series markets, Kalshi CFTC contracts, Bovada and specialist esportsbook receipts are de-vigged proportionally and weighted (0.36 / 0.24 / 0.18 / 0.12 / 0.10). A specialist receipt older than 24 hours is rejected instead of being displayed as a live market, and the rejection is printed on the row.
  3. Originated line where no book prices the match. Chronological Elo over every recorded Leaguepedia series since MatchSchedule.DateTime_UTC >= '2024-09-01 00:00:00', margin-weighted, with inactivity decay, read at each fixture's own kick-off.
  4. Provisional and unresolved. Fewer than five rated series on either side is published as provisional; official mid-split removals (dqteams) are quarantined on Unresolved instead of being given a fake number.

Formulas

Expected series win probability for team A:

EA = 1 / (1 + 10^((RB - RA)/400))

Chronological rating update after each completed series, with the series game margin from Leaguepedia (base K = 48.0, margin weight = 0.45):

Keff = K · (1 - w + w · |gameswon - gameslost|)
R′A = RA + Keff · (SA - EA)

Inactivity decay starts after 45 days and reaches the 1500 prior at 450 days. Ratings used for a pick are snapshotted at that fixture's kick-off, after decaying to that moment, using only results recorded before it. No post-hoc shrinkage is applied: it measurably hurt the holdout. Probabilities are clamped to 0.05–0.95.

Out-of-sample backtest & calibration

Every recorded series after 2026-07-01 (3,854 out-of-sample series) is predicted strictly before its result updates either team's rating. Hyper-parameters were chosen on an earlier 2026-04-01+ window and then checked on this later holdout; the adopted setting is the one reported here.

Hard guardrails

  • Zero Hotspawn crowd input. Crowd pick shares are never read by the probability engine.
  • Zero look-ahead. A fixture's own result is excluded from the ratings used for it; the 52 already-played cards that carried a recorded result in the previous build were re-priced from their pre-kick-off snapshot, and the publish gate now asserts it.
  • Zero placeholder odds. Symmetric 1.80 / 1.80 aggregator defaults are discarded.
  • Blank beats invented. A value that cannot be confirmed is left blank and listed on Flagged for review.
