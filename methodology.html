The 4-tier probability architecture

  1. Inventory. Every League of Legends fixture is pulled from Hotspawn's public fixtures API (play.hotspawn.com/lol-pickems).
  2. Tier 1 — Multi-venue real-money consensus. Live odds and contract books from Pinnacle (0.36), Polymarket full-series CLOB (0.24), Kalshi CFTC (0.18), Bovada (0.12), are de-vigged proportionally and averaged using fixed editorial weights (not statistically optimized).
  3. Tier 2 — Originated regional Elo lines. All recorded Leaguepedia series since MatchSchedule.DateTime_UTC >= '2024-09-01 00:00:00' are processed chronologically. Best-of-one series move a rating by K = 48.0; best-of-three and longer series move it by K = 40.0 scaled by the margin of victory (multiplier = 1 + 0.5 · ln(1 + |game margin|)). Inactivity decay begins after 45 days and reaches 1500 at 450 days.
  4. Tier 3 — Provisional & Unresolved quarantine. Thin-sample teams (<5 series) are marked provisional; teams reported removed mid-split (dqteams) are quarantined in Unresolved rather than mispriced.
  5. Automated publish gate. Every 6 hours GitHub Actions re-runs the full pipeline and executes strict integrity assertions (Verification) before publishing to GitHub Pages.

Formulas

Expected series win probability for team A:

EA = 1 / (1 + 10^((RB - RA)/400))

Chronological rating update after each completed series:

R′A = RA + Kbo · M · (SA - EA)

Kbo = 48.0 for best-of-one, 40.0 for best-of-three and longer. M = 1 for best-of-one; for multi-game series M = 1 + 0.5 · ln(1 + |score margin|).

No shrinkage. Pulling small samples toward 1500 was measured out-of-sample and made the probabilities worse (Brier 0.2176 with shrinkage versus 0.2150 without, n = 3,854), so thin samples are labelled provisional instead of being shrunk.

Inactivity decay begins after 45 days and reaches 1500 at 450 days; originated probabilities are clamped to 0.05–0.95.

Out-of-sample backtest & calibration

Every recorded series after 2026-07-01 (3,854 out-of-sample series) is predicted strictly before its result updates either team's rating (zero look-ahead leakage).

How the update sizes were chosen. Kbo, M and the (now disabled) shrinkage were selected on the disjoint validation window 2025-07-01..2026-07-01 (16,289 series, Brier 0.2217), then frozen. Hard methodological guardrails

  • Zero Hotspawn crowd input. Crowd pick shares are never read by the probability engine.
  • Zero placeholder odds. Symmetric 1.80 / 1.80 default placeholders on odds aggregators are automatically discarded.
  • Explicit quarantine over guesswork. Disqualified teams (dqteams) and unrated cards are documented transparently on Unresolved and Flagged for review.

Independent research dashboard. Rows link to source records. Leaguepedia is community-maintained; model lines are estimates, not verified facts. Zero Hotspawn crowd-pick input.
