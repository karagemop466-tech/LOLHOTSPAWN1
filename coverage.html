Read this first

Every one of the 241 cards on Hotspawn's League of Legends pick'em lobby is mirrored here and carries a probability. Only 9 of them are priced by real bookmakers, because real bookmakers only price the international events. 163 carry our own Elo line and 65 are provisional (thin data); 4 are deliberately unresolved and listed on Unresolved with their limitations.

This page is the audit: it shows where the external evidence exists, what it said when it was captured, how far it sits from the number we published, and — for the competitions with no book coverage at all — the evidence that no reachable book prices them.

Receipts are corroboration only: they are read after the picks are built and cannot change a pick, a probability or a method. A price that is not in a receipt is never inferred.
