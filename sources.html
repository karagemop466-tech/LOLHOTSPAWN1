How every match was chased, in order

  1. Established sportsbooks — Pinnacle, Bovada, DraftKings, FanDuel, BetMGM, Betway, Unibet/Kambi, BetOnline, BetUS, MyBookie were all probed on this build; the ones that answered are listed above, the ones that refused are listed with their HTTP code.
  2. Esports-native books and exchanges — Polymarket, Kalshi (CFTC), Thunderpick, Rivalry, Luckbox, BC.GAME, GG.BET were probed the same way.
  3. Odds aggregators — EGamersWorld, Oddspedia, OddsPortal, BetExplorer, EsportsBets, VegasInsider, Action Network and The Odds API were probed for a LoL feed.
  4. Our own line — any match still unpriced gets an Elo probability, and the row says so. 5 of 14 probed endpoints refused the runner on the last build, so a missing price is never treated as a 50/50 match.
  5. Settlement or unresolved — a fixture that cannot be played as listed is published as a settlement pick with no probability.

Why most Hotspawn matches have no bookmaker price

The books we can reach quote the events they choose to hang a market on, which in practice means tier-1 and tier-2 series. National divisions (Prime League 2nd/3rd Division, RIVALS League, Komplettligaen, LigaGG, Icelandic Esports League and similar) were checked on every build and returned no matching market in any reachable book. That is a coverage fact about the books, not a missing step in the pipeline — and it is why those matches carry an originated Elo line instead of a market line.

Prop markets are never used as win probabilities

Pinnacle returns kill, map and handicap props in the same feed as the moneyline, marked only by the participant name (“G2 (Kills)”). Those rows are dropped before team matching and counted in the build audit, because a kill handicap is not a probability that a team wins the series.
