  • Pinnacle returns 138 matchup objects for its one League of Legends league, of which 8 carry an open, two-sided match-winner moneyline. The parallel (Kills) market family is a kill total and is excluded everywhere.
  • Kalshi lists 8 open KXLOLGAME contracts and Polymarket carries 12 unsettled two-sided LoL markets in the same snapshot; both are nearly all Worlds 2026.
  • That ceiling — about a dozen series across all reachable venues — is why most of the Hotspawn list is originated by our own model rather than quoted: the books simply do not list national divisions, academy leagues or regional splits.
