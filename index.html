How a number gets on this page

  1. Inventory — the full LoL list is pulled from Hotspawn's public fixtures API, not scraped from the page.
  2. Market first — if a reachable book or exchange quotes the match, we remove the bookmaker margin on each venue and average the venues.
  3. Our own line second — otherwise an Elo rating fitted on 3,854+ community-recorded series produces the probability, flagged provisional when the sample is thin.
  4. Blank last — when neither exists, the row stays blank and is listed above.
