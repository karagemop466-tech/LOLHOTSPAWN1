Unresolved picks and the limitations (4)

These cards cannot carry a playable win probability. They stay in the list to mirror Hotspawn exactly, they are reported separately here in the summary, and they are never padded with a guessed number. Where the league itself has ruled a forfeit, the official settlement winner is shown — it is not a forecast. Hotspawn's published rule voids forfeited-match predictions, with no points awarded or deducted. Full ledger, per-card detail and every limitation: Unresolved & limitations.

  • Why a card can have no line. A team officially removed mid-split (dqteams) may have its remaining fixtures voided or graded as walkovers, so a probability built from its earlier results would be fiction. An official forfeit record settles the pick; a competitive percentage would be invented, so none is published.
  • No market on national and academy divisions. 225 of 234 matches are not priced by any book reachable from the runner; their lines come from recorded series history rather than an aggregator placeholder. The competition-by-competition ledger is on Unresolved & limitations.
  • What we tried for the low-tier tail. 23 further esportsbook and odds-aggregator endpoints were called from the build runner on 2026-10-25 14:07 UTC: 0 returned a machine-readable LoL price (5 blocked or key-gated, 8 browser-only pages, 10 error or empty responses).   • Thin & zero-history debut samples. 71 lines are labelled provisional because at least one side has fewer than five rated series in the 25-month window (3 involve a debut side with 0 pre-kickoff rated series, including 0 where both sides enter at 0/0 rated series and rest solely on equal division priors). They still carry a pick, with the confidence stated; they are not treated as equal to a market line.
