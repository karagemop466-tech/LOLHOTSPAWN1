Executive summary

  • Coverage. 264 matches across 27 competitions are listed in the Hotspawn LoL pick'em lobby right now. The full list, refreshed from the lobby every 4 hours, is on Picks and Unresolved.
  • Priced matches. 9 matches have a verifiable external market (Bovada, Pinnacle, Polymarket). Each pick shows the probability, the books used, the raw decimal odds and a link to the source.
  • Unresolved matches. 255 matches have no external market at all — mostly amateur and semi-pro leagues (e.g. PRM 3rd Division, Tunisian Super League, Icelandic league). They are listed with their limitation on Unresolved; no number is invented for them.
  • Not used as an input. Hotspawn's crowd pick percentages and Hotspawn's own picks never enter a probability. Only external sportsbook prices and prediction-market prices do.
  • Method & audit. De-vigging, source blending and matching rules are documented on Method; per-source status is on Sources; open irregularities are on Flagged.
