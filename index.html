Executive summary

  • Where the picks are. On a phone, tap Picks in the bar at the bottom of every page: one row per match with the pick, both win probabilities, the method and source links. Card view shows the same 241 matches with the reasoning behind each line, and Results grades the picks that have already played. Every list is searchable and filterable by tournament and by method. Next kick-offs are under Next up.
  • Coverage. 241 of 241 matches carry a pick (100%): 237 with a win probability and 4 resolved to the official forfeit winner with no probability claimed. Priced: 9 market-backed (Bovada, Kalshi, Pinnacle, Polymarket, Unibet), 150 model and 78 provisional.
  • Where the lines come from — and where they do not exist. We probed 45 external venues from the build runner (established books, esportsbooks, exchanges, odds aggregators); 21 answered and 11 returned JSON. On this build the feeds that actually quoted a listed match were: Pinnacle 8 series, Bovada 8 series, Unibet (Kambi) 8 series, Kalshi 8 contracts, Polymarket 12 unsettled markets — overwhelmingly the same Worlds 2026 ties. Everything below that ceiling has no external line to quote, so we originate it ourselves. Probe table and receipts: Sources.
  • Extra sources tried for the low-tier tail. 17 further esportsbook and odds-aggregator endpoints were called from the build runner on 2026-10-24 16:20 UTC: 0 returned a machine-readable LoL price (3 blocked or key-gated, 4 browser-only pages, 10 error or empty responses). Those leagues therefore keep the originated line rather than a borrowed or invented quote. probe receipt · Sources.
  • Unresolved picks & limitations. 4 cards cannot carry a playable probability today; they are listed with their reason and source in the section below and in full on Unresolved & limitations. Nothing is padded with a guessed number.
  • Independence and limits. Hotspawn's public pick percentages are captured for audit only and carry zero weight in any probability (see Method). Market quotes are source observations, not official verification; originated lines are model estimates from community-maintained Leaguepedia history; a Hotspawn forfeit settlement is unverified — see Flagged for review.
  • Verification. 12 of the 18 polled feeds answered on the last build, every row links to its official source, and the 42-check publish gate (Verification) blocks publication if a row lacks a source, the two probabilities fail to sum to 100%, or an internal link breaks.
