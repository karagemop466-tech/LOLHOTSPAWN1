Executive summary

  • Where the picks are. On a phone, bookmark Pick table - one row per match with the pick, both win probabilities, the method and source links. Card view shows the same 241 matches with the full reasoning. Both pages are searchable and filterable by tournament and by method.
  • Coverage. 241 of 241 matches carry a pick (100%): 237 with a win probability and 4 resolved to the official forfeit winner with no probability claimed. Priced: 9 market-backed (Bovada, Kalshi, Pinnacle, Polymarket, Unibet), 151 originated and 77 provisional.
  • Where the lines come from — and where they do not exist. We probed 33 external venues from the build runner (established books, esportsbooks, exchanges, odds aggregators); 15 answered and 8 returned JSON, of which five are live match-price feeds. Across every reachable venue the same slice of the calendar is quoted: Pinnacle 8 series, Bovada 7 series, Unibet (Kambi) 8 series, Kalshi 8 contracts and Polymarket 12 unsettled markets — overwhelmingly the same Worlds 2026 ties. Everything below that ceiling has no external line to quote, so we originate it ourselves. Probe table and receipts: Sources.
  • Evidence limits. Market quotes are source observations, not official verification; originated lines are model estimates from community-maintained Leaguepedia history; archived sportsbook receipts are excluded from pricing; and the Hotspawn settlement of a forfeited card is unverified - see Flagged for review.
  • Strictly independent of Hotspawn crowd votes. Hotspawn's public pick percentages are captured for audit comparison only and carry zero weight in any probability - see Method & out-of-sample backtest.
  • Verification. 10 of the 14 feeds the collector polls answered on the last build (Sources — a separate, wider probe of 33 venues including esportsbooks and aggregators is published there too). Every row links to its official source, and the 40-check publish gate (Verification) blocks publication if a row lacks a source, the two probabilities fail to sum to 100%, or an internal link breaks.

Unresolved picks and the limitations (4)

These cards cannot carry a playable win probability. They stay in the list to mirror Hotspawn exactly, are reported separately, and are never padded with a guessed number. Where the league itself has ruled a forfeit, the official settlement winner is shown - it is not a forecast.
