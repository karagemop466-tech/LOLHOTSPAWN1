Unresolved picks and the limitations (4)

These cards cannot carry a playable win probability. They stay in the list to mirror Hotspawn exactly, are reported separately, and are never padded with a guessed number. Where the league itself has ruled a forfeit, the official settlement winner is shown - it is not a forecast. Hotspawn's published rule voids all predictions on a forfeited match (no points either way), so expect these cards to score zero whichever side you tap (Hotspawn — How Pick'ems Works).
