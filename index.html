Executive summary

  • Coverage: 241 of 241 matches carry a pick (100%). 237 of those are priced with a probability and 4 are settlement-only. Priced picks: 9 market-backed from external bookmaker and exchange lines, 148 originated model lines and 80 provisional lines. The full list in kick-off order is All picks.
  • Which matches have a real bookmaker line, and which we had to originate. External line coverage lists every competition with its method, the external price we captured for it (with the source page and capture time) and the evidence that no reachable bookmaker prices the rest.
  • Unresolved picks and the limitations on producing them (4 matches). These are the 4 Komplettligaen 2026 Autumn fixtures involving Solid Gaming, which the community-maintained Leaguepedia record Data:Komplettligaen/2026 Season/Autumn Split shows removed in Round 10. Each of these four fixtures matches a Leaguepedia series that carries its forfeit flag (FF=1 or FF=2, meaning Solid Gaming forfeited) with a recorded 0–2 score in favour of the opponent; the flag is not universal — eight of Solid Gaming's eleven series carry it, three do not — so it is stated per match, never assumed (Leaguepedia FF query receipt). Hotspawn still lists them as scheduled, so we publish the administratively correct side (the active opponent) and no win probability: a competitive number cannot be justified for a series that may be voided or awarded as a walkover. Every such match, plus the limitations that apply to the rest of the list (no bookmaker market below tier A, thin samples, one-sided history, disconnected opponent graphs, geo-blocked books), is on Unresolved & limitations.
  • External lines only, with a model cross-check. Market rows are built from Pinnacle, Polymarket, Kalshi (CFTC) and Bovada, de-vigged proportionally and precision-weighted; archived esportsbook receipts expire after 24 hours so an old price can never masquerade as a live line, and quotes that imply more than 97% on one side are rejected as non-actionable. Our own Elo probability is printed beside every market price so a disagreement is visible (Market). Hotspawn crowd shares are stored for audit only. Method and out-of-sample backtest: Method.
  • Refresh and verification. The pipeline reruns every 6 hours and re-reads Hotspawn's fixture list on every run, so new cards appear without any manual step. 9 of 14 probed feeds answered on the latest runner build (Sources), and 31 automated publish gates must pass before anything is committed (Verification). Irregularities are published on Flagged for review.
