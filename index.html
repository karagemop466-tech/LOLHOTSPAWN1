  • Where the lines come from — and where they do not exist. We probed 45 external venues from the build runner (established books, esportsbooks, exchanges, odds aggregators); 20 answered and 11 returned JSON. On this build the feeds that actually quoted a listed match were: Pinnacle 8 series, Bovada 8 series, Unibet (Kambi) 8 series, Kalshi 8 contracts, Polymarket 12 unsettled markets — overwhelmingly the same Worlds 2026 ties. Everything below that ceiling has no external line to quote, so we originate it ourselves. Probe table and receipts: Sources.
  • Extra sources tried for the low-tier tail. 17 further esportsbook and odds-aggregator endpoints were called from the build runner on 2026-10-24 16:20 UTC (1xBet / 22Bet / Melbet / Betwinner LineFeed, Rivalry, DraftKings, GG.BET, Thunderpick, Midnite, BetOnline, OddsPortal, BC.GAME, Abios, PandaScore): 0 returned a machine-readable LoL price (3 blocked or key-gated, 4 browser-only pages, 10 error or empty responses). Those leagues therefore keep the originated line rather than a borrowed or invented quote. probe receipt · Sources.
  • Evidence limits. Market quotes are source observations, not official verification; originated lines are model estimates from community-maintained Leaguepedia history; archived sportsbook receipts are excluded from pricing; and the Hotspawn settlement of a forfeited card is unverified - see Flagged for review.
  • Strictly independent of Hotspawn crowd votes. Hotspawn's public pick percentages are captured for audit comparison only and carry zero weight in any probability - see Method & out-of-sample backtest.
  • Verification. 12 of the 18 feeds the collector polls answered on the last build (Sources — a separate, wider probe of 45 venues including esportsbooks and aggregators is published there too). Every row links to its official source, and the 41-check publish gate (Verification) blocks publication if a row lacks a source, the two probabilities fail to sum to 100%, or an internal link breaks.
