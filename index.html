Executive summary

  • Coverage. 264 of 264 matches have a pick. 9 come from market prices (Pinnacle moneylines, plus Polymarket where a live market exists); 187 come from our Elo model and 68 are provisional model lines with thin data.
  • Unresolved. 0 match(es) cannot be priced from any verified source; they are listed on Unresolved with the exact reason, never guessed.
  • Independent of Hotspawn. Hotspawn's public pick percentages are not used as inputs anywhere in the maths - see Method.
  • Sources. 7 of 12 checked data sources responded; the rest are documented as blocked on Sources.
  • Refresh cadence. A GitHub Actions job re-pulls Hotspawn, Pinnacle and Leaguepedia every 6 hours and republishes this site. Snapshot time is shown at the top of every page.
  • Flagged for review. Data irregularities and unverified items are collected on Flagged for review.
