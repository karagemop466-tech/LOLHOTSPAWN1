Executive summary

  • Where the picks are. On a phone, tap Picks in the bar at the bottom of every page: one row per match with the pick, both win probabilities, the method and source links. Card view shows the same 234 matches with the reasoning behind each line, and Results grades the picks that have already played. Every list is searchable and filterable by tournament and by method. Next kick-offs are under Next up, and Picks by tournament jumps straight into a filtered pick table for one league.
  • Coverage. 234 of 234 matches carry a pick (100%): 230 with a win probability and 4 resolved to the official forfeit winner with no probability claimed. Priced: 9 market-backed (Bovada, Kalshi, Pinnacle, Polymarket, Unibet), 150 model and 71 provisional.
  • Where the lines come from — and where they do not exist. We probed 45 external venues from the build runner (established books, esportsbooks, exchanges, odds aggregators); 21 answered and 11 returned JSON. On this build the feeds that actually quoted a listed match were: Pinnacle 8 series, Bovada 8 series, Unibet (Kambi) 8 series, Kalshi 8 contracts, Polymarket 12 unsettled markets — overwhelmingly the same Worlds 2026 ties. Everything below that ceiling has no external line to quote, so we originate it ourselves. Probe table and receipts: Sources.
  • Extra sources tried for the low-tier tail. 23 further esportsbook and odds-aggregator endpoints were called from the build runner on 2026-10-25 14:07 UTC: 0 returned a machine-readable LoL price (5 blocked or key-gated, 8 browser-only pages, 10 error or empty responses). Those leagues therefore keep the originated line rather than a borrowed or invented quote. probe receipt · Sources. A separate 52-venue low-tier probe (2026-10-25) is documented in the research memo and receipts; see the competition-by-competition fallback ledger.
  • Bookmaker evidence for the matches nobody prices. 17 checks across 9 venues (Betmonitor, Cloudbet, EGamersWorld, GG.BET, OddsPortal, Rivalry, Thunderpick, Vave, bo3.gg LoL match centre) were read by hand on 2026-10-25 and logged with the exact sentence each page printed. They are on Unresolved & limits → what the books showed: the leagues below the featured ceiling carried no open price at any of them on the dates shown, which is why those cards are originated rather than quoted.
  • Unresolved picks & limitations. 4 cards cannot carry a playable probability today; they are listed with their reason and source in the section below and in full on Unresolved & limitations. Nothing is padded with a guessed number.
  • Independence and limits. Hotspawn's public pick percentages are captured for audit only and carry zero weight in any probability (see Method). Market quotes are source observations, not official verification; originated lines are model estimates from community-maintained Leaguepedia history; any walkover grade instead of Hotspawn's published void rule remains conditional — see Flagged for review.
  • Verification. 12 of the 18 polled feeds answered on the last build, every row links to its official source, and the 51-check publish gate (Verification) blocks publication if a row lacks a source, the two probabilities fail to sum to 100%, or an internal link breaks.

Unresolved picks and the limitations (4)

These cards cannot carry a playable win probability. They stay in the list to mirror Hotspawn exactly, are reported separately, and are never padded with a guessed number. Where the league itself has ruled a forfeit, the official settlement winner is shown - it is not a forecast. Hotspawn's published rule voids forfeited-match predictions, with no points awarded or deducted.
