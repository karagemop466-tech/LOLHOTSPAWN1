How a number gets on this page

  1. Inventory — the full LoL list is pulled from Hotspawn's public fixtures API, not scraped from the page.
  2. Market first — if a reachable book or exchange quotes the match, the margin is removed on each venue and the venues are precision-weighted. Prop markets (kills, maps, handicaps, totals) are rejected before matching.
  3. Our own line second — otherwise an Elo rating fitted on 3,854+ community-recorded series produces the probability, flagged provisional when the sample is thin.
  4. Settlement or blank last — a removed participant gets a settlement pick with no probability and appears in the section above.
