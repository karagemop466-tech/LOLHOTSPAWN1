Executive summary

  • Where the updated picks are. The single complete list is All picks (241 rows, searchable, next kick-off first). This page's Next 48 hours block is the fastest check on a phone. Method splits are on Market-backed (9) and Model lines (228).
  • Coverage. All 241 fixtures in the Hotspawn LoL pick'em are listed; 237 have a pick (98%). 9 are priced from de-vigged Pinnacle, Polymarket, Kalshi, Bovada and specialist esportsbook quotes. 228 regional/academy matches have no reachable sportsbook market, so their line is originated from chronological Leaguepedia series results, taken strictly at kick-off so a played match can never see its own result.
  • Unresolved picks and limitations (4). 4 Komplettligaen fixtures involve Solid Gaming, removed mid-split under the official Leaguepedia dqteams record. Hotspawn may void them or grade walkovers, so no win probability is valid. Every unresolved fixture, the walkover/void caveat, the 79 thin-sample caveats and the list of books that were tried and blocked are on Unresolved & limitations.
  • Independent of Hotspawn votes. Hotspawn's pick percentages are stored only for audit comparison (they are exposed as crowd traps on Flagged) and carry zero weight in any probability. Method, formulas and the 3,854 series out-of-sample backtest are on Method.
  • Freshness and sources. Rebuilt every 6 hours by GitHub Actions (Sources, 9/14 probed feeds live). This pass also probed 31 bookmaker, esportsbook and odds-aggregator endpoints from the runner: 13 answered HTTP 200, and none of the reachable esportsbooks served server-rendered LoL prices. That evidence, including blocked books, is on Unresolved & limitations.
