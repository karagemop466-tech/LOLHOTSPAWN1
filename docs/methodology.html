Method

How each number on this site is produced, and what we refuse to do.

Data snapshot: 2026-10-21 20:50 UTC · refreshed automatically every 6 hours

The pipeline

  1. Inventory. Every League of Legends fixture is pulled from Hotspawn's own pick'em fixtures API (the same JSON that renders play.hotspawn.com/lol-pickems).
  2. Market prices. Pinnacle's esports feed is filtered to League of Legends, then each matchup's moneyline is pulled. Polymarket is used only where a live two-sided market exists.
  3. History. Every series result since MatchSchedule.DateTime_UTC >= '2024-09-01 00:00:00' comes from Leaguepedia's Cargo export of MatchSchedule, the community-maintained results database for LoL esports.
  4. Our line. Elo is fitted over series results in chronological order, then decayed for inactivity and shrunk toward the mean for teams with few results.
  5. Publish. The site is regenerated and verified by CI, then committed to GitHub Pages.

Formulas

Expected score for team A:

EA = 1 / (1 + 10^((RB - RA)/400))

Rating update after a series (K = 24.0):

R′A = RA + K · (SA - EA)

Shrinkage towards the 1500 start rating for a team with n rated series:

Reff = 1500 + (R - 1500) · n / (n + 5.0)

Time decay begins after 45 days without a series and reaches the 1500 baseline after 450 days.

Probabilities are clamped to 0.05-0.95.

What we deliberately do not do

  • We never use Hotspawn's crowd pick percentages as an input. They are captured in the raw data for transparency only.
  • We never use Hotspawn's own editorial predictions as a probability.
  • We do not fill in a missing price with a guess. Missing data means a provisional line with the limitation printed on the card, or an unresolved entry.
  • We do not blend bookmaker and model numbers into one figure. Each row says which method produced it.

Refresh

A scheduled GitHub Actions workflow runs every 6 hours (cron 17 */6 * * *), re-collects all sources, rebuilds the dataset and this site, runs the verification gate, and commits. The timestamp at the top of each page is the snapshot time in UTC.
