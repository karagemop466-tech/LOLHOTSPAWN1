Methodology, Walk-Forward Calibration & Venue Audit

Full technical specification of the 6-hour scheduled Hotspawn LoL ingestion pipeline, Shin power de-vigging, 5-venue consensus weighting, strict pre-kickoff Elo/Bradley-Terry ratings, and competition-scoped entity disambiguation.

1. Strict Independence from Hotspawn Signals

Hotspawn's public API exposes participants0_picked_percent, participants1_picked_percent, offered_points, and tier. Zero Hotspawn crowd or gamification fields are ever read by any probability, consensus, or rating function in scripts/analyze.mjs. Every published win probability derives solely from external real-money markets or external historical match outcomes.

3. Self-Originated Pre-Kickoff Elo / Bradley-Terry Model

  • Dataset: 32,536 completed non-forfeit Leaguepedia + Hotspawn LoL series replayed in strict chronological order; administrative forfeits (ff) are excluded from skill updates.
  • Zero look-ahead: Every Hotspawn fixture at timestamp T is scored exclusively from ratings accumulated across matches with t < T.
  • Competition-scoped entity disambiguation: Teams are resolved inside their specific Leaguepedia tournament page before global matching, preventing cross-region homonym contamination (such as Bulgarian amateur PNG vs Brazilian paiN Gaming on #1083097, or Swiss PvB (Swiss Team) vs Vietnamese Phong Vũ Buffalo on #1079031).
  • Empirical-Bayes shrinkage & series transform: Ratings initialize at 1500 with dynamic K = 38 / 28 / 22, shrink toward 1500 via n / (n + 6), and map to Bo3/Bo5 series probabilities with correlated-game damping (α = 0.72). Full current ratings JSON: data/node/model/ratings.json ↗.
