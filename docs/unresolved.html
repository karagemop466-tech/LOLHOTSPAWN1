  • Sportsbook coverage ceiling on grassroots ERLs: Regulated esportsbooks and prediction markets price Tier-1 (Worlds) and select Tier-2 (LGC Rising) events (~4% of scheduled volume); the remaining ~93% rely on self-originated pre-kickoff Elo models, which carry wider epistemic uncertainty (OOS Brier 0.2251).
