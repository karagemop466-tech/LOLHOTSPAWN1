Why a match can be unresolvable

  • No market coverage. Established esports books (Pinnacle and the sportsbooks aggregated by odds sites) price tier-1/tier-2 events; amateur and regional divisions (e.g. PRM 3rd Division, RIVALS LEAGUE, Tunisian Super League, Icelandic Esports League, Arabian Regional Championship) are not offered.
  • No prediction-market coverage. Polymarket lists only high-profile LoL matches (Worlds, major regional leagues).
  • No history for our own line. Our Elo-style model is built from real results in the Hotspawn archive. If either team has never played a recorded series, the model has nothing to rate — outputting 50/50 (or any number) would be fabricated precision.
  • No manual input. Per project requirements, nothing is filled in by hand or guessed; unresolvable matches stay unresolvable until a source appears (each refresh re-checks all sources).
