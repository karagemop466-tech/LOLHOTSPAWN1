## 4. Irregularities found

### F1 — The LCP 2027 Promotion line was not reproducible from its source (high, now resolved)

At the 11:43Z snapshot, `#1081423 Ground Zero Gaming vs Frank Esports` was published at **91.1% / 8.9%**,
sourced from an archived EGamersWorld listing reading of `1.025 / 10.5` captured `2026-10-21T20:36Z`
(`archive/session-8336fb7e/data/book_receipts.json`). On 2026-10-23:

- the same site's **match page** publishes the symmetric `1.8 / 1.8` placeholder
  → <https://egamersworld.com/lol/match/eDTgthgYk/ground-zero-gaming-vs-frank-esports-wvOLKhrveF>
- its **listing row** still shows `1.025 / 10.5` → <https://egamersworld.com/lol/matches>

A two-day-old aggregator reading that the source's own match page contradicts is not evidence for a 91%
line. PR #18 removed the archived receipt, and the card is now a **model** line at Frank Esports 77.5%
(Ground Zero 22.5%) — a **68.6 pp swing** that was driven entirely by that one unverifiable reading. Which
side the archived prices belonged to cannot be confirmed from the source page either, so neither reading
is used. The card is published as `conflicting / not reproducible` on `coverage.html`, both readings are
quoted there, and the archived venue is excluded from corroboration.

### F2 — "N-Venue Consensus" labels a two-day-old quote as a live venue (medium)

9 published rows were labelled `5-Venue Consensus` only because an archived `Esportsbook` quote from
`2026-10-21T20:36Z` was still loaded from `archive/session-8336fb7e/data/book_receipts.json`. The label
implied a live fifth venue; the site showed no capture time. Now itemised, with quote and capture time,
in `coverage.html` §5, and excluded from the corroboration table.

### F4 — One past kick-off is not marked (low)

`#1071212 Rich Gang vs Phenti` (15:00Z) is past kick-off at 15:40Z but is not flagged "start time passed",
because that flag is computed at build time (11:43Z) and the cron runs every six hours. Up to six hours
of matches can therefore look open. Fix belongs in `tools/site.py` (compare `start_utc` with the render
clock, not the build clock) — logged as an integration request, not edited here because `site.py` is
owned by two other active sessions.

### F5 — Coverage limitation, published rather than papered over (informational)

231 of 241 cards have no bookmaker price. Independent confirmation that no reachable book prices them:

- OddsPortal's LoL section lists exactly one competition: "League of Legends World Championship (8)"
  → <https://www.oddsportal.com/esports/league-of-legends/>
- EGamersWorld's LoL coupon prices only 2026 World Championship, LGC 2026 Rising and LCP 2027 Promotion
  → <https://egamersworld.com/lol/matches>
- bo3.gg's match centre lists only the 8 Worlds matches → <https://bo3.gg/lol/matches/current>
- Pinnacle's guest API returns LoL leagues for the World Championship only, from the runner
  → <https://guest.api.arcadia.pinnacle.com/0.1/sports/12/leagues>

For PRM 2nd/3rd Division (102 cards), Tunisian Super League (25) and RIVALS LEAGUE (28) no bookmaker
line exists to import, so an originated line is the only honest answer, and the method is disclosed on
every card.
